SHADOW LEDGER

One thief. One archive. Five guards who never blink.

Slip through the Meridian Archive after dark. Steal the 3 ledger pages, then reach the green exit without being caught.

  • WASD / Arrow keys — move  ·  click a floor tile to walk there
  • Hold Shift — sneak: slower, quieter, harder to spot
  • Bushes hide you · crates & walls block a guard's sight
  • Shadows (dark floor) halve how fast you are spotted
  • P pause · R restart · M mute

If a guard's cone turns red and the alarm fills, break line of sight and wait — suspicion fades. Get caught and the run is over.
