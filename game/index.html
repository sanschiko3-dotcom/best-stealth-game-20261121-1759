SHADOW LEDGER

One thief. Three heists. Guards, cameras, and a vault that never sleeps.

Slip through the Meridian Archive, the Hall of Portraits and the Star Sapphire Vault. Steal the 3 ledger pages on each floor, grab the gold, then reach the green exit — finish a heist without being caught for a +$500 ghost bonus.

  • WASD / Arrow keys — move  ·  click a floor tile to walk there
  • Hold Shift — sneak: slower, quieter, harder to spot
  • C / right-click — toss a coin: a guard walks over to look
  • Q — throw a smoke pellet: cones cannot see through smoke
  • H — field notes: legend, patrol routes and callouts
  • Bushes hide you · crates & walls block a guard's sight
  • Shadows (dark floor) halve how fast you are spotted
  • Cameras sweep the Gallery and the Vault — stay out of their cones
  • Caught 3 times and the run is over — a strike sends you back to the door
  • P pause · R restart · M mute

If a guard's cone turns red and the alarm fills, break line of sight and wait — suspicion fades. Get caught and you lose a strike, not the run.
